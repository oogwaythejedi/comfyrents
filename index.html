Good questions

Before you book

Is instant booking really instant?

Yes — you get confirmation within seconds. Some stays need host approval for special requests.

What payment methods work?

eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, bank cards and cash at the property for select hosts.

Are the reviews verified?

Every review is tied to a confirmed booking. Hosts can't pay or trade for reviews.

Do you offer long-term stays?

Yes — monthly rates for apartments and guest houses are listed with a 'long stay' badge.

What if a stay doesn't match its listing?

Message us within 2 hours of check-in. We rebook or refund — that's our guarantee.
