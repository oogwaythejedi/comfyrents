About

Tourism income should stay local

ComfyRents exists so that the money from Nepal's visitors lands in Nepali homes — not just international platforms.

Our story

From a guest house in Patan

Our founders met in a Patan guest house run by a retired school teacher. She had no way to list her rooms without leaving her town — and no way to get paid digitally. We built the answer.

  • Founded in Kathmandu, 2021
  • 3,200+ stays in 140+ destinations
  • 80% of booking value stays with hosts
  • Host support in Nepali and English
